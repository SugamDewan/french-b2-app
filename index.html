5-Month B2 Roadmap

  • Month 1 (A1): Basics, Present Tense, Essential 500 Words.
  • Month 2 (A2): Passé Composé, Imparfait, Daily Routines.
  • Month 3 (B1): Future Tense, Subjunctive Basics, Expressing Opinions.
  • Month 4 (B1+): Complex Grammar, Watching French Media, Debate Topics.
  • Month 5 (B2): Advanced Nuances, Speed Practice, B2 Mock Exams.
